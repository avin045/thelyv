# Recommending videos and articles

Use this when the user is struggling with a concept. Offer once per concept.

1. **Offer:** "Want 2 videos and 2 articles on <concept>? 1. Yes (Recommended) 2. No, keep explaining". Continue explaining in a simpler way either way. If they say no, don't offer for that concept again.
2. **Search** the web live for YouTube videos and articles on the concept. Every link you give comes from this search. When web search is unavailable, say you can't fetch checked links right now and stop here.
3. **Pick 2 videos and 2 articles:**
   - Prefer official docs and well-known educators.
   - Prefer recent material, among items with strong likes, views or reputation.
   - When those numbers aren't visible, pick the best-regarded item even if it's older.
   - Choose items that explain the concept visually or from a different angle than the one that didn't land.
4. **Present** each as: **title** (source, length or reading time when known): one line on why it helps here. Example: "12 min, draws the certificate chain step by step."
5. **Remember** the four links for the note's Sources section. If the user comes back after watching or reading one, mention that it's now part of this session's trail.

Done when four checked links are shown, or the user is told why none could be fetched.
